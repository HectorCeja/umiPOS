# Product Polish Research

Date: 2026-07-15. Human acceptance remains **FAIL — PRODUCT EXPERIENCE**.

## Feedback received

The owner did not understand why Sales, Cash and Mailpit were presented as separate destinations; could not discover a real onboarding or obvious logout; encountered English and infrastructure language; and judged the visual result closer to a styled technical console than a commercial SaaS. The result is preserved, not rewritten.

## Principles extracted

- Shopify Admin/Polaris: one merchant home, task-based information architecture, the fewest useful categories, stable navigation, breadcrumbs and action-led labels.
- Square: the dashboard behaves as business headquarters, with performance and operational tools gathered into one context.
- Stripe: constrained tokens and consistent components create quality; brand expression should be deliberate rather than arbitrary per screen.
- Apple HIG, Material 3 and Fluent 2: clarity before novelty, visible system status, hierarchy through typography/space, motion that explains change, and accessible interaction states.
- Linear, Notion, Raycast, Vercel and Framer: fast search, calm density, progressive disclosure and careful microcopy make complex systems feel learnable.
- Behance POS references: commercial desire comes from editorial composition, confident imagery, rhythm, depth and strong focal actions—not from copying decorative dashboards.

## Rejected patterns

- Multiple “start here” URLs.
- Infrastructure tools beside business modules.
- Generic grids of equal cards, decorative metrics and fake activity.
- Visible UUIDs, internal event vocabulary and English domain nouns.
- Dark sidebar plus purple button as the complete brand system.
- Photography of food without operational meaning; excessive blur, neon or continuous animation.

## Conceptual moodboard

“Warm operational atelier”: midnight ink for trust; terracotta/coral for human hospitality; cream and paper neutrals for calm; mint for healthy operations; tactile ceramic, brushed metal and restrained translucent materials. Editorial headlines pair with compact operational typography. Abstract business imagery joins café, inventory and cash as one connected system.

## Flutter continuity

The palette, semantic colors, radii, typography hierarchy, icon rules, motion durations and Spanish vocabulary are tokenizable. Flutter should inherit these principles while using platform-native navigation and touch behavior; this work does not change UMI ownership or contracts.

# Responsive Strategy

Official coverage is 360×800, 390×844, 430×932, 768×1024, 820×1180, 1024×768, 1280×800, 1440×900 and 1920×1080. Reflow at 200% is treated as a narrow viewport, not as a scaled desktop.

Global rules use tokenized gutters and control heights. Schedules, role cards and product galleries collapse to one column; no selector requires hover. Operational tables must become labeled records before they would produce horizontal scrolling. Safe-area padding protects mobile controls.

# UMI Cash Integration Foundation

Future public events are `nexo.cash.session.opened`, `.closed`, `.force_closed`, `.variance.detected`, `.withdrawal.recorded`, `.deposit.prepared` and `.transfer.completed`. Payloads contain IDs, version/time, Organization/Branch/Register/Session, business date, currency and relevant summary amounts/status/correlation only—never full ledger or PII. `UmiCashIntegrationPort` remains disabled/local-sink until definitive UMI contracts exist.
